# Records of the corpus opfsql answers differently from SQLite, and why
# (README.md). Each record is `path:line reason`, the line being that of
# the record's first line in the original file; each reason is a section of
# docs/semantics.md opfsql follows there, or a feature it does not have.
# A record listed here that passes fails the suite: take it out.

# Departures, documented in docs/semantics.md.
reason bare-column semantics.md, Queries and Departures: a column neither grouped nor aggregated is an error; SQLite reads a bare column of some row
reason compare-types semantics.md, Comparisons: operands must be comparable; TEXT or BLOB against INTEGER is a bind error, where SQLite converts by affinity
reason assigned-twice semantics.md, Departures: assigning one column twice in an UPDATE is a bind error, where SQLite takes the rightmost assignment
reason sum-avg-text semantics.md, Aggregates: sum and avg take numbers only; over TEXT they are a bind error, where SQLite reads the text as a number

# Features opfsql does not have.
reason desc-index not supported: ASC or DESC on an index key
reason in-table not supported: IN with a table name, x IN t1
reason in-empty not supported: an empty IN list, x IN ()
reason from-parentheses not supported: parentheses around FROM items
reason indexed-by not supported: INDEXED BY and NOT INDEXED
reason total not supported: the total() aggregate
reason group-concat not supported: the group_concat() aggregate
reason shift not supported: the << operator, and the sums over the rows it would have inserted
